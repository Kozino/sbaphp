A PROPOSED CONSTITUTION FOR THE “CLASS
OF 2012”, ST. JOHNBOSCO SEMINARY,
ISUANIOCHA
PREAMBLE:
We, the members of the Class of 2012, St. John Bosco Seminary Isuaniocha , having
resolved to exist in unity and harmony as one indivisible and indissoluble
association, and to provide a constitution for the purpose of promoting the good
government and welfare of all persons in the association, on the principles of love,
justice, equality and fairness, and for the purpose of consolidating the unity we
profess, do hereby make, enact and give to ourselves the following constitution:-
PART ONE:
Section One:
1. NAME: The association shall be known  and addressed as Bosco Class of
2012

Section Two:
1. AIMS AND OBJECTIVES:
The aims and objectives of the association shall include:
a. To act as a medium through which good and cordial relationship is
developed and maintained among members.
b. To give identity to and identify with every member of the association
always.
c. To lend helping hands to members, financially and otherwise.
d. To encourage the goodwill and active participation of all members.
e. To act as a midwife for the all round development of members as good
citizens of Nigeria and ambassadors of the Church

2. POWERS:

In furtherance of the objectives, but not otherwise, we state that:-
a. The power of the association resides primarily with the general assembly,
hereinafter, known as the members of the association.
b. The executives are elected from and are answerable to the members of the
association.
c. The executives albeit, reserve the power to:-
i. Publicize and promote the work of the association and organ ize the
Annual General Meetings, training courses, events and seminars, etc.
ii. Invite and receive contributi ons, and raise funds where appropriate,
to finance the work of the association, and to open a bank account to
manage such funds
iii. Work with associations and associations of a similar nature and
exchange information, advice and knowledge with them, including
cooperation with other voluntary bodies, charities, statutory and non-
statutory organizations.
iv. Appoint or set up committees as are necessary to conduct activities
to meet the objectives,
v. Take any form of action that it lawful, which is necessary to achieve
the objectives of the association.
vi. Give out loans and other forms of help to members, albeit,
regulatory.

  Section Three:
1. MEMBERSHIP:

a. Membership shall be open to anyone who entered St. Dominic Savio
Seminary, Akpu in the year 2006, as a  freshman (JSS 1 student) and has
either continued with the set, or left the seminary without joining another
set.
b. Membership shall also be open to persons that joined the seminary
debutants of 2006 thereafter.
c. It shall also, in case of any marriage valid before the law and the Church ,
be extended to the spouse of the member, by virtue of association.
d. Where it is considered that membership will be detrimental to the aims and
objectives of the association , the executives shall have the power to refuse
membership to applicants.
e. The management committee may, by resolution passed at a meeting
thereof, terminate or suspend the membership of any member, if in its
opinion, his/her conduct is prejudicial to the interests and objectives of the
association, PROVIDED THAT  the individual member shall have the
right to be heard by the general assembly before the final decision is made.
f. Any member of the association may resign his/her membership by
providing the association via the Secretary with a written notice.

PART TWO:
Section One:
1. MANAGEMENT COMMITTEE:
 The association shall be administered by a management committee of not less
than three (3) perso ns and not more than fifteen (15) persons, who prior to
their election, must be financially up to date with the association. The
executives shall be elected for a period of two (2) years, but may be re -elected
at the association’s Annual General Meeting.

2. EXECUTIVES:
a. The association shall have a management committee consisting of:
i. President
ii. Vice President
iii. Secretary
iv. Assistant Secretary
v. Financial Secretary
vi. Treasurer
vii. Provost
viii. Public Relations Officer

3. FUNCTIONS OF THE EXECUTIVES:
a. THE PRESIDENT shall:
i. Function as the leader of the association, and the head of the
management committee.
ii. Preside over the Annual General Meeting and in any case, all other
activities of the association.
iii. Arrange for all meetings.
iv. Set up and inaugurate sub-committees and panels.
v. Be one of the signatories to the association’s bank account.
vi. In matters of contention, have the final say.
vii. Take decisions on behalf of the association, in the face of pressing
matters and act as official representative of the association to outside
persons and agencies.
viii. Offer assistance in an advisory capacity when necessary to other
executive members
ix. Sign all official documents and papers on behalf of the association.
x. Establish quorum when necessary.

b. THE VICE PRESIDENT shall:
i. Act in the place of the President, in the absence or incapacity of the
President.
ii. Work together with the President and executives.
iii. Gather current data (address, phone numbers) on executive members
annually and distribute to the executive members and committee heads.
iv. In conjunction with the organizers, arrange the meeting place.

c. THE SECRETARY shall:
i. Be the custodian of those records specifically assigned which include:
• The minutes of all Executive Committee and Annual General
Meetings
• The minutes of all Standing Committees and Special Committees.
ii. Record the minutes of all general and executive meetings.
iii. Read all papers that may be called for by the meeting.
iv. Be responsible for giving notice of meetings and circulating facts and
minutes of all proceedings to the executive.
v. Be responsible for corresponding with individuals or organizations.

d. THE ASSISTANT SECRETARY shall:
i. Keep roll of members present at meetings and call roll when required.
ii. Stand in for the Secretary when absent or incapacitated.

e. THE FINANCIAL SECRETARY shall:
i. Keep accurate record of all financial transactions of the association.
ii. Receive funds.
iii. Issue receipts of membership fees to members.
iv. Submit a financial report at each Annual General Meeting of the
association.
v. Be one of the signatories to the association’s bank account.
vi. In conjunction with the President  and Treasurer, draft the association
budget.

f. THE TREASURER shall:
i. Keep all cash assets of the association.
ii. Make expenses on behalf of the association.
iii. Keep accurate record of the financial transactions of the association.
iv. Liaise with the Financial Secretary  and President in drafting the
association’s budget.
v. Be one of the signatories to the association’s bank account.
vi. Approve the financial report before it is read at each Annual General
Meeting.

g. THE PROVOST shall:
i. Maintain decorum in the meeting place.
ii. Penalize offenders and collect their fines accordingly.
iii. Be the returning officer on any in-house voting.

h. THE PUBLIC RELATIONS OFFICER shall:
i. Be in charge of circulation of memo and notices within the association.
ii. Shall be the liaison between the association, relevant agencies and
news media.
Section Two:
1. MEETINGS:
i. There shall be an Annual General Meeting on the 31 st of December, every
year, unless otherwise stated.
ii. Meetings shall enable members to discuss actions and monitor progress to
date, and to consider future developments.
iii. All members shall be given at least fourteen (14) days’ notice  of when a
meeting is due to take place.
iv. It shall be the responsibility of the President to chair all meetings, or in his
absence, the Vice President or any other designated deputy.
v. All members are entitled to vote at the Annual General Meeting. Voting
shall be made by a show of hands on a majority basis. In the case of a tied
vote, the President or an appointed deputy shall make the final decision.
vi. One is considered late for a meeting if he comes after the minutes might
have been read.
vii. One is considered absent from the meeting if he comes after the exhaustion
of the day’s agenda.

2. FINANCE:
i. Any money acquired by the association  including donations, contributions
and bequests, shall be paid into an account operated by the management
committee in the name of the association.
ii. Any money obtained by the association shall be used only for the
association.
iii. Only one bank account shall be opened in the name of the association, and
that shall be with the United Bank of Africa (UBA).
iv. Every member of the association shall pay an annual due of N1000 before
the end of September, each year.
v. At every Annual General Meeting, a sum of N 500 will be paid as an
appearance fee. N200 shall b e charged for absenteeism, N100 will be
charged for lateness, while N50 will be charged for noisemaking.
vi. The signatories to the  bank account shall include the President , the
Financial Secretary and the Treasurer.
vii. Any income or expenditure shall be the responsibility of the Treasurer who
will be accountable to ensure funds are utilized effectively and that the
association stays within the budget.
viii. An annual financial report shall be presented at the Annual General
Meeting.

3. BENEFITS:
    The members shall be entitled to the following benefits, as and at when due:
i. Right to vote and be voted for GIVEN THAT he or she is financially up to
date with the association.
ii. On cases of serious illness of a financially up-to-date member made known
to the association, a visit of the association, with a cash gift of N 2000,
unless otherwise agreed by the executives.
iii. On cases of direct bereavement of a financially up- to-date member, a visit
of the association with a cash gift of N5000 unless otherwise agreed by the
executives.
iv. On cases of ordination, religious profession, marriage ceremony and
anniversary of financially up- to-date members, a visit of the association
with a cash gift of N5000
4. MISCELLANEOUS:
i. The association shall be represented at every event of a member, in
which she is officially invited.
ii. In the case of the bereavement of a direct family member of a member
of the association, a cash gift of N1000 shall be presented to the family,
and N 4,000 presented to the member.
iii. In the case of a major sickness of any member, the association shall
visit the said member with a cash gift of N 2,000.
iv. The above mentioned privileges are only for those who do not owe  the
association in any way.

PART THREE
Section One:
1. AMENDMENT OF CONSTITUTION:
i. Any change to this constitution must be agreed by a majority vote at
an Annual General Meeting.
ii. Amendments to this constitution or dissolution of the association
must be conveyed to the Secretary formally in writing. The Secretary
and other executives shall then present it to the AGM to discuss such
proposals.

2. DISSOLUTION OF THE ASSOCIATION:
i. The association may be dissolved if deemed necessary by the
members in a majority vote at a special meeting. Any assets or
remaining funds after debts have been paid shall be returned to the
providers or transferred to local charities or similar groups at the
discretion of the Management Committee.
